# D-016 — v1 ships no platform plugins

**Decided by:** the Product Owner, at G5, on the review committee's finding.
**Supersedes nothing. Constrains:** `FR-M4-04`, `FR-M4-05`, `FR-M9-05`.

## The decision

KODO v1 ships with **no Flutter plugin that requires an Android permission**. That closes
out, for v1:

- importing a backdrop from the gallery or the camera (`FR-M4-04`);
- importing or recording a sound (`FR-M4-05`);
- capturing thirty seconds of screen (`FR-M9-05`).

Each stays *In progress* in the requirement register with this decision named, rather than
being quietly dropped or left to look like an unfinished task.

## Why

The G5 review measured something nobody had claimed: the release Android manifest declares
**no permissions at all**. Not reduced, not justified — none. The operating system will not
let the process open a socket, read a photo, or turn on a microphone. KODO's central promise
is that a child's work goes nowhere, and on Android that promise is currently kept by the
kernel rather than by a code review.

Every one of the three requirements above adds a permission, and with it a dialogue a
nine-year-old is asked to agree to and a parent is asked to trust. The consent gate for
each is already built and tested; what is missing is the plugin, and the plugin is the
part that costs the guarantee.

Against that: a child can already make a backdrop by drawing one, make a sound with
`tambour` and `note`, and share a drawing as an SVG or a PNG. Nothing in the curriculum
needs a camera. The three requirements buy polish; the permission set buys the product's
defining property.

## What this is not

It is not "we ran out of time". If v1.2 adds any of the three, the decision to spend the
permission is taken then, deliberately, with the privacy note rewritten — and the test in
`app/test/m19_acceptance_test.dart` that reads the manifest will fail until somebody does.

## How it is enforced

`app/test/m19_acceptance_test.dart` parses `AndroidManifest.xml` and fails if a
`<uses-permission>` element appears. `tools/verify_findings.dart` checks the same thing as
finding `G5-08`, so a plugin added in passing fails the build rather than the next audit.
